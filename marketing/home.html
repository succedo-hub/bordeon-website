01 / YOUR EXISTING INFORMATION

Bring a representative example.

Walk through an anonymised Excel file or system export with us: policy references, agreed premium, commission and carrier shares. Together we identify the source of each field.

02 / ONE CONNECTED RECORD

Record the change once.

Enter an agreed endorsement and premium adjustment against the policy. Bordeon retains the previous version and calculates commission, net premium and carrier allocation from the recorded amounts and shares.

03 / TRACEABLE OUTPUT

Reuse the transactions in the report.

Generate a CSV bordereau from unreported transactions. Keep the saved report and its carrier obligations linked to the policy history, without rebuilding the same calculations in separate files.

How do our existing files and systems fit?

We agree the source of record, field mapping, transfer method and responsibilities before implementation. The interactive demo uses manual entry of accepted business and agreed adjustments. Any file ingestion, integration or carrier-specific output is explicitly scoped and confirmed for your setup.

Which work does Bordeon take out of the process?

Once the transaction is recorded, commission and carrier shares flow into the report and its obligations. Earlier policy versions and report snapshots stay available, so your team can trace an amount without reconstructing it from separate versions of a spreadsheet.
