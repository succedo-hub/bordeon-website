THE COMPANY

Insurance knowledge.
A shared transaction record.

The operational problem.

Policy changes, premium adjustments and carrier reports often live in separate files. Each handover creates work to establish which version applies, how an amount was derived and what the carrier should receive.

The Bordeon solution.

Bordeon connects policy history, premium movements and reporting at transaction level. It gives MGAs and brokers a consistent record to follow from accepted business through changes and carrier obligations.

The experience behind the company.

Our founding team brings Nordic insurance experience, including work on the carrier side and with the underlying insurance information. That experience shapes how we approach the details of insurance operations and counterparty handovers.

Our ambition.

Establish a shared transaction foundation for specialty insurance. Extend it through reusable reporting templates, connections to existing systems and long-term relationships with customers and partners.
