THE INVESTMENT CASE

A specific operational problem.
A wider infrastructure ambition.

The problem we are starting with.

Policy changes, premium adjustments and carrier reports often live in separate files. Each handover creates work to establish which version applies, how an amount was derived and what the carrier should receive.

The product we are building.

Bordeon connects that history at transaction level. We are starting with one binder’s path from accepted business to reporting, then extending the same model through reusable carrier templates and connections to finance.

The commercial model we intend to validate.

Paid, scoped pilots followed by recurring software subscriptions. Expansion comes from additional binders, reporting relationships and workflows within the same customer. Pricing and onboarding effort will be tested with pilot customers.

The experience behind the company.

Our founding team brings Nordic insurance experience, including work on the carrier side and with the underlying insurance information. We are turning that experience into a product for the teams responsible for policy changes, premium records and reporting.
